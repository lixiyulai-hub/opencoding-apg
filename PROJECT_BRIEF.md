# OpenCoding Project Brief

品牌名：OpenCoding
仓库 slug：opencoding-apg
兼容别名：Adaptive Project Governance（adaptive-project-governance）、APG

## Problem and users
OpenCoding 是 APG-only 的项目治理与 AI coding 工作流测试夹具；它验证从中文想法到计划、任务编排、离线执行器契约、证据和回滚的可审计流程。

## Outcomes and scope
- Desired outcome: 用 OpenCoding 品牌呈现可重复、可验证的 APG 工作流证据。
- In scope: repository contents discovered at initialization.
- Out of scope: global activation, unrelated repositories, and unapproved structural migration。

## Evidence tasks
- Confirm users, ownership, lifecycle, and release model。
- Replace unknown data and exposure classifications with evidence。
